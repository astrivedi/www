Ashutosh Trivedi

Associate Professor of Computer Science
University of Colorado Boulder

I work on formal methods for reinforcement learning, trustworthy AI, and safety-critical software and cyber-physical systems.

My research combines verification, learning, and symbolic reasoning to make intelligent systems safer, fairer, and easier to explain.

News

  • Sep 2026
    Received the 2026 Outstanding Faculty Research Advisor/Mentor Award from CU Boulder’s College of Engineering and Applied Science.
  • Jun 2026
    Launched the three-course Foundations of Reinforcement Learning Specialization through CU Boulder and Coursera, covering classical RL, deep RL, and reward programming.
  • Jun 2026
    Our paper BarrierBench: Evaluating Large Language Models for Safety Verification in Dynamical Systems appeared at L4DC 2026. It introduces a benchmark of 100 dynamical systems and an LLM-agentic framework for barrier-certificate synthesis.
  • Apr 2026
    Towards Persistent Noise-Tolerant Active Learning of Regular Languages with Class Query, with Lekai Chen and Alvaro Velasquez, appeared at ICLR 2026. The paper introduces CAPAL for active automata learning under persistent noise.
