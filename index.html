Selected Contributions

  1. Learning with symbolic structure

    Regular Reinforcement Learning

    How can reinforcement learning work with sets of states, rather than individual states alone? We use regular languages to represent these sets and symbolic transformations to describe transitions, connecting reinforcement learning with ideas from model checking.

    CAV, 2024CAV Distinguished Paper Award

    Paper (PDF) · Publisher · BibTeX

  2. Beyond safety guarantees

    Closure Certificates

    Showing that a system avoids unsafe states is only part of verification. Closure certificates reason about transitions across time, extending certificate-based verification to richer temporal requirements for dynamical systems.

    HSCC 2024

    Paper (PDF) · arXiv · Publisher · BibTeX

  3. Finding discrimination in the extremes

    Fairness Testing through Extreme Value Theory

    Average fairness measures can hide severe disadvantage for some individuals. We use extreme value theory to estimate worst-case discrimination and guide mitigation, making the tails of a model’s outcomes part of its fairness assessment.

    ICSE, 2025

    Paper (PDF) · arXiv · BibTeX
