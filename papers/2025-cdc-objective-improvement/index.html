Objective Improvement Algorithm for Controller Synthesis in Uncertain Environments

Daniele Dell’Erba, Sven Schewe, Ashutosh Trivedi.

CDC, 2025

Paper (PDF) · Publisher · BibTeX

Summary

The Objective Improvement Algorithm computes optimal values and policies in stochastic games by repeatedly solving linear programs. The method treats both players symmetrically. The paper establishes correctness and convergence and reports experiments on its practical performance for controller synthesis.

Ashutosh Trivedi · University of Colorado Boulder
