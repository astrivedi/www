KV-Fold: One-Step KV-Cache Recurrence for Long-Context Inference

Alireza Nadali, Patrick Cooper, Ashutosh Trivedi, Alvaro Velasquez.

Unpublished preprint (arXiv), 2026

Paper (PDF) · arXiv · BibTeX

Summary

KV-Fold is a training-free protocol for long-context inference that carries a key-value cache across successive sequence chunks. Each chunk attends to the accumulated cache and appends its own keys and values. The preprint investigates the stability of this recurrence and its ability to retain information across long contexts.
