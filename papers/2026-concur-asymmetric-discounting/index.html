Asymmetrically Discounted Stochastic Games

Sarvin Bahmani, Soumyajit Paul, Sven Schewe, Shadi Tasdighi Kalat, Ashutosh Trivedi.

CONCUR, 2026

Paper (PDF) · Publisher · BibTeX

Summary

We study stochastic games in which players use different discount factors. Optimal strategies may require memory and randomization. Computing incentive Stackelberg equilibria is no harder than solving classical discounted games, while restricting players to stationary strategies makes the problem computationally intractable.
