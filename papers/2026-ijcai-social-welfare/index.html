Social Welfare under Heterogeneous Time Preferences

Sarvin Bahmani, Soumyajit Paul, Sven Schewe, Shadi Tasdighi Kalat, Ashutosh Trivedi.

IJCAI–ECAI 2026 (to appear)

Paper (PDF) · arXiv · BibTeX

Summary

We study social welfare in Markov decision processes with multiple principals whose reward functions and discount factors differ. Welfare-maximizing strategies can require memory, but pure finite-memory counting strategies suffice and can be synthesized in polynomial time. Restricting strategies to be positional makes threshold questions NP-hard.
