\cvsection{Research Profile}
I develop mathematical foundations and tools for \textbf{trustworthy AI systems that learn and make decisions under uncertainty while satisfying formal guarantees}. My research brings together reinforcement learning, formal verification, and symbolic reasoning, with applications to safety-critical control and accountable software.

\vspace{2bp}
{\fontsize{9.2}{11.1}\selectfont
\textbf{Research themes:} reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making; logic, automata, and formal verification; stochastic systems and control; fairness and accountability; learning-enabled cyber-physical and medical systems.\par}
